Tulip Street

Ye daastaan daaram ke mikham begam.
I have a story I would like to tell.

Kalameye "tulip" az faarsiye baastaan miad, vali na az kalameye "laleh".
The word tulip comes from ancient Farsi. But not from the word "laleh".

Tooye Iraane baastaan, oonaa fekr mikardan ke in gol shabihe ye ammameye koocholooe.
In ancient Persia yhey thought the flower looked like a little turban.

Oonaa az kalameye ghadimie faarsiye "dulband" estefaade mikardan.
They used the old Persian word "dulband".

Tooye Torkiye in kalame shod "tülbent".
In Turkie the word became "tülbent".

Tooye Aalmaan in kalame shod "tülpe".
In Germany the word became "tülpe".

Pas be engelisi mishe "tulip".
Then "tulip" in English.

Pas esme khiaaboonetoon az ye kalameye faarsi miaad.
So your street name comes from a persian word.
